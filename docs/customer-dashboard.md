# What customers see

In **Your assistants**, select a business and **Open customer dashboard**. Choose Today, 7 days, 30 days, or an inclusive pair of business dates. Tap **?** beside any number for its source and denominator. “—” means unavailable, not zero. Check **Updated … min ago**, the timezone and dates published before comparing two figures. With fewer than 20 inbound conversations, compare counts first.

| Section | A useful first question |
|---|---|
| Overview | How many customers wrote? How many needed an owner? Compare the trailing week with the previous seven dates. Attributed revenue and time saved are estimates, not bank receipts or a time sheet. |
| Agents & activity | Which specialist handled or handed off a chat? Filter hourly activity by type, agent, masked contact, local hour and outcome. See numeric weekly analyst reports as they were sent, not regenerated prose. |
| Leads | Which inquiries became quotes or paid orders? Filter the current stage of leads created in the selection. Unknown labels are aliases; a free-form staff name or customer tag never becomes report text. |
| Payments & decisions (owner) | What is still waiting, overdue, under recheck or rejected? Tap an order number for the recorded decisions and changes. **Nothing on this page can accept a payment.** Use the existing WhatsApp owner commands. |
| Usage | Where did calls, tokens and failures go? Look at the daily/monthly charts and provider, model, key generation, agent and kind tables. The optional calculator uses **only the owner’s own rate**, in this browser tab. |
| Analytics | Where does conversion drop? Check denominators, acquisition sources, busiest hours, question topics, objections, experiments, guard detections and review queue. A blocked duplicate is not a double reply delivered. |
| Health | What did the worker last observe about its link, key state, incidents, decrypts and delivery acknowledgements? Transport counters reset at worker restart. |
| Export & data (owner) | Download JSON or CSV for the selected dates. Lead CSV is also available on Leads. Follow **account data controls** for the existing export-before-deletion flow. |

**A brief is different from a report.** Owners can choose **Open brief**, then follow the short-lived link on their own HTTPS tenant live-inbox origin. Sign in to that inbox separately with its owner dashboard token. If no origin is configured, enter only your inbox origin, **not a token**, on the platform. Return to the tenant brief tab and select **I’m signed in — open brief**. Only the tenant serves the collected field values. Staff cannot issue a brief link, open Payments, export tenant reports, or change the bot. Staff retain their own personal account portability/deletion rights.

**If the worker is offline:** cached figures remain available with their original timestamp. No snapshot or partial history is not evidence of zero activity. Backfill covers the tenant records still available, up to the plan window; it cannot reconstruct information deleted before reporting was enabled.
